Frequently asked

FAQ

How do I report a bug?

Email riderhunter301@gmail.com with your Android version, device model, app version, a description of the issue, steps to reproduce, and screenshots if you have them.

How do I request a new feature?

Send your idea by email. Describe what you'd like the app to do and why it would help you, and the team will review it.

How do I contact support?

Email is the primary and fastest support channel. Reach RH Studio at riderhunter301@gmail.com.

How do I report an incorrect PDF or OCR result?

Email us with your device model, app version, the feature you were using (such as OCR, merge, split, or compress), and, if possible, a screenshot or description of the issue.

How do I request privacy information?

Email riderhunter301@gmail.com with your privacy question and the team will get back to you directly.

Does RH Studio provide phone support?

No. RH Studio currently only offers support by email, which is the most reliable way to reach the team.

How long does it take to receive a response?

RH Studio aims to respond within 48–72 business hours, though this may vary during weekends or holidays.

Before you write

Support guidelines

Including the following details helps RH Studio resolve your issue faster:

  1. Device name — e.g. Samsung Galaxy A54
  2. Android version — e.g. Android 14
  3. Application version — found in the app's settings screen
  4. Problem description — what happened, and what you expected instead
  5. Steps to reproduce — what you did right before the issue occurred
  6. Screenshots — optional, but very helpful
