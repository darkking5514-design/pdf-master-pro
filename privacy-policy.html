1 Introduction

PDF Master Pro ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android document utility application that helps users merge, split, compress, convert, protect, scan, and extract text from PDF and document files. This Privacy Policy describes, in plain language, what information the app may process, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to the PDF Master Pro Android application and to this Privacy Policy webpage. It does not apply to other companies' or developers' websites or applications, even if linked from within the app.

2 Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, PDF Master Pro may process the following general categories of information:

  • Files and documents you select, create, or export (e.g., PDFs).
  • Images you choose for conversion, such as Image to PDF operations.
  • Camera captures made when you use the document scanner feature.
  • OCR-related content, meaning text extracted from a document or image you select.
  • Device and technical information, such as general device or app diagnostic details, where applicable to app functionality or advertising.
  • Advertising identifiers, processed by Google AdMob and its partners as described in Section 6.
  • IP address, which may be processed by Google AdMob and its partners for advertising delivery and security purposes.
  • Approximate location information, which may be derived from technical signals such as IP address for advertising purposes, where applicable.
  • Advertising interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed, or interacted with.

Not every category listed above is necessarily collected from every user, every time. What is processed depends on the feature you use, your device, your region, applicable law, and your consent choices where consent is required.

3 Your Files and Documents

PDF Master Pro may access files that you explicitly select or create in order to perform the operation you request, such as PDFs, images, scanned documents, generated PDF files, and text extracted through OCR.

PDF Master Pro does not claim ownership of your documents. You remain responsible for the documents you choose to process using the app, including ensuring you have the right to process them.

Processing model: All PDF, image, and OCR processing in PDF Master Pro happens entirely on your device. Your files, images, and OCR text are not uploaded to or processed on any server operated by us.

4 App Permissions

The permissions requested by the app may depend on the Android version and the specific features you use. Potential permissions include:

Camera

Used when you choose the document scanner feature, to capture an image of a physical document.

Photos / Media

Used when you select images for PDF creation or other related operations.

Files / Storage

Used to select, open, save, or export documents supported by the app.

The app requests permissions only when relevant to the feature you are using, and does not use the camera to monitor you continuously or outside of an active scanning session that you initiate.

5 How We Use Information

Information described in this policy may be used to:

  • Provide PDF and document processing features, including merge, split, compress, and Image to PDF
  • Perform OCR / text extraction that you request
  • Support the document scanner feature
  • Apply protection (such as a password) to a PDF you create
  • Save and export the results you request
  • Provide advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Measure or improve advertising performance, where applicable
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Maintain and support core app functionality
  • Diagnose technical problems, if applicable
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

PDF Master Pro does not currently use analytics or crash-reporting tools such as Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be revised to disclose the tool and the information it processes.

6 Advertising and Google AdMob

PDF Master Pro uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google, to display advertisements within the app.

PDF Master Pro uses the following AdMob-supported advertising formats:

Banner Ads Native Ads Rewarded Ads

Not all formats are necessarily displayed to every user or at every point in the app; which formats appear depends on the app's configuration and the screen or feature you are using.

Depending on applicable laws, your consent choices, your device settings, SDK configuration, and the type of advertisement served, Google and its advertising partners may process certain advertising-related or technical information, which may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location information
  • App information
  • Ad interaction information
  • Diagnostic or technical information
  • Information related to consent and privacy choices
  • Information needed to deliver, measure, and protect advertising

PDF Master Pro does not provide your documents, files, images, or OCR text to Google AdMob or its advertising partners. Advertising-related technical information (described above) is separate from, and does not include, the contents of the documents you process in the app.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's own privacy resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Data from Partner Sites and Apps
Google UMP: PDF Master Pro does not currently implement the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another consent management tool. If this changes in a future update, this section will be updated to describe the consent mechanism in place.

7 Rewarded Advertisements

PDF Master Pro uses a rewarded advertisement flow for certain eligible PDF-generation operations. Where this applies, the general flow is as follows:

  1. You perform an eligible PDF operation.
  2. You reach the step where the result is about to be generated.
  3. A rewarded advertisement may be presented.
  4. You watch or complete the rewarded advertisement.
  5. Your PDF or result is then generated.

Rewarded advertisements are not required for every feature in the app. OCR / Extract Text does not use the rewarded advertisement flow — you are not required to watch a rewarded advertisement to use OCR / Extract Text.

As with all AdMob ad formats, rewarded ads may involve the processing of advertising-related technical information as described in Section 6.

8 Privacy Choices and Consent

Advertising privacy choices available to you may vary based on your location, applicable privacy laws, your consent status, your device settings, and AdMob's configuration. Where applicable, you may be offered choices relating to personalized or non-personalized advertising.

Personalized advertising is not necessarily used for every user, and a consent form is not necessarily shown to every user — this depends on your region and applicable legal requirements.

Where a consent mechanism is presented, it will allow you to make choices about advertising personalization to the extent required by applicable law and supported by the app's configuration.

9 Third-Party Services

PDF Master Pro currently uses the following confirmed third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — advertising service, see Section 6.

Third-party providers, including Google, have their own privacy practices governing how they handle information, which are described in their own privacy policies (see the Google links in Section 6).

10 Data Sharing

Information may be shared with third-party service providers when necessary to provide their services, including advertising services such as Google AdMob, as described in Section 6.

We do not share the contents of your PDF documents, images, scanned files, or OCR output with advertisers. Advertising-related technical information (such as advertising identifiers or ad interaction data) is separate from your document content.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not claim that no information is ever shared, because the use of a third-party advertising service like Google AdMob inherently involves the processing of certain advertising-related information by Google.

11 Data Security

We may use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to help protect information associated with the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot promise perfect or absolute security.

If you use the Protect PDF feature, we recommend using a strong, unique password. The strength of a password-protected PDF's security depends significantly on the password you choose.

12 Data Retention

PDF Master Pro does not store your files on any server. Files that you create, scan, or save using the app remain on your device or your chosen storage location until you delete them. If server-side storage is introduced in a future update, this section will be updated to describe the applicable retention period.

13 Data Deletion

  • PDF Master Pro does not currently provide server-side storage for users' PDF files. Files saved on the device or selected storage location can be deleted by the user at any time.
  • PDF Master Pro does not currently require users to create an account to use its core PDF-processing features, so there is no account-based deletion process to describe.
